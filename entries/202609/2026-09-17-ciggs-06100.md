Why am I having an anxiety attack at 3A.M?

Story time today!

Ever since I was a small small small little tiny kid (which is basically infant), I've known that I dislike smokes. Born with a respiratory system basically for decoration, and a wonderful nasal allergy that just keeps on striving, even the faintest foreign substance would trigger a never-ending sneeze streak, along with bunch of tears and sometimes a few rash spots for extra flavor. And randomly on top of that, my acoustic mind simply decided that my most important sense would be smell. Everytime I eat something, walk into a room, or meet someone new, my first instinct is to smell them (yes I do that unconciously and conciously, and you may or may not realize 🫣). That's also why I spend too much of my adult money on perfumes, and pay regular visits to stores on sad days (ok I will re-visit this topic some random day, please be patient whoever you are). Okay back to the story, you get why I don't like smokes, all kinds. They are just too potent, and linger for too long. Every time I get back from a billard session, or a net session, I would feel like puking and want to burn all the clothes :\. Luckily, no one in my close circle smokes that much, and even if they do, they have the decency to clean themselves properly and doesn't reek. Bafflingly, my house in Hà Nội always has the strongest scent of smoke from chả nướng, and I also don't like that at all (love you mom).

Flavor is also a huge factor in my decision to consume things. If you know me, you would know that I'm a huge sucker for anything that tastes good (good is very very VERY objective). I love my pretentious specialty coffee, I'm hugely interested in tasting raw foods, and don't get me started on the wonders of the world like ramen, curry,.. Hell, as much as I hate being drunk, I still drink *kinda* regularly because I love the unique taste alcohol can bring. With that, I knew from the start that ciggs are just not for me. They either taste of nothing, or too chemical, no in-between. Furthermore, they also harm your taste bud, hindering the underlying taste, and affect your appetite.

I hit my first cigg in my 3rd year of uni, on a random rooftop on Hoàng Quốc Việt. It was ... uninteresting, basically the same as I expected, I felt absolutely nothing aside from a small cough and the lingering smell. I couldn't finish it all because of the taste and stopped at 2/3 or sth. It was a purple Craven and I did not know how to pop the capsule lmao. Then, I did not touch a single more until a short time before graduation. If I remember correctly, my second smoke was at a coffee shop, where I basically spend 7 hours a day just horsin around, talk to others and procrastinate on finishing my thesis. I was also newly unempl*yed back at the time, so too much free time, not enough pressure. I took a smoke from the folks there and still, didn't like it at all. It still tasted bad, and felt like nothing, but it was a nice way to socialize, and an easy way to watch people on close up. And the same goes for my 3rd, 4th,.. (not very regular, they smoke once a day, and I only join them like once a week or even less). This pretty much stopped when I moved to Sài Gòn 3 years ago.

Then I went through almost 2 smoke-less years in here. No friends smoked, no colleagues smoked (in front of me), and I don't go to coffees that much anymore. Though my life was still a mess, there was simply no urge to smoke anymore, not until that one week. Now I don't recall the faintest memory that there was any big event happened then, but I remember feeling absolutely empty, needing something to boost myself up the whole week, so I just randomly bought a pack. I think I only smoked like 4 from that, once a week, and then gave the rest of the pack away. I still despised the taste, and nicotine still didn't hit me, but the act of smoking seemed so liberating, exactly what I needed then.

I continued to smoke once or twice a week, then quit for bout a month, then just relapsed and backed to buying a bunch of random packs but can never completely finish them, not until recently.
I thought that if nicotine were to affect you, it would numb your mind, comfort you, help you forget things.
So why does it just make me feel more..? The breath of relief when you light up, the short comfort during the middle, and the shivering sensation at the end. Why does the feeling linger, why does my mind scream, why does my heart ache?  
and this feeling is getting addictive.
